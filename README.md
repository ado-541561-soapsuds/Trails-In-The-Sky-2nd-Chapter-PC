## 🕹️ About

Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter is a story-driven Japanese role-playing game developed by Nihon Falcom and set in the fantasy kingdom of Liberl. Following Joshua's disappearance, Estelle Bright journeys across Liberl to uncover his past and confront the secretive Ouroboros organization. The adventure combines exploration, character-focused storytelling, side quests, and strategic battles that shift between real-time Quick Battles and command-based combat. Released on September 16, 2026, it expands the original journey with full 3D presentation, expressive event scenes, new combat options, and an even broader narrative.

A strong fit for players who enjoy heartfelt JRPG stories, detailed world-building, strategic party combat, and long-form character arcs.

## ✨ Features

- 💥 **Brave Rush** — Link party members into powerful real-time combination attacks that can break enemy defenses.
- 🗺️ **A Living Liberl** — Travel across a richly detailed kingdom filled with towns, roads, landmarks, and evolving stories.
- 📖 **Character-Driven Story** — Follow Estelle's emotional journey through a deeply connected narrative of friendship, identity, and redemption.
- 🛩️ **Expansive Adventure** — Explore a larger journey with substantial side content, optional objectives, and returning locations.
- 🌟 **Powerful Crafts** — Build energy through battle and unleash distinctive Crafts and S-Crafts.
- 🎬 **Full 3D Presentation** — Experience Liberl through modern 3D environments, expressive character performances, and cinematic scenes.
- ⚔️ **Hybrid Combat** — Switch seamlessly between fast Quick Battles and tactical command-based encounters.
- 🔮 **Orbment System** — Customize characters with quartz and use Arts to shape your party's strengths and tactics.
